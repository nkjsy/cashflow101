## Short description

## Description

Ratrace is a turn-based money strategy game about breaking free from living paycheck to paycheck.

Pick a profession and a dream, then roll your way around the Daily Grind. Every space brings a decision: snap up a rental or a small business, sell into a hot market, weather a layoff, or borrow to grab a great deal before a rival does.

Once your passive income beats your expenses, you break out onto the Freedom Lane. Run bigger businesses, protect yourself from risks, and race to afford your dream before the AI does.

Features:
- Quick games (about 12 minutes) or full-length games (about 35 minutes)
- 1–3 AI rivals, three difficulty levels and distinct AI personalities
- 8 professions with unique skills and 8 dreams to chase
- Guided mode explains each rule the first time you meet it
- Everyone's finances are public, so you always know where you stand
- Autosave, so you can pick up where you left off
- Play in English or Chinese

## Controls

- Mouse or touch: click or tap the on-screen buttons to roll the dice, pick deals, buy, sell, borrow and end your turn.
- Keyboard: Tab moves between buttons and Enter presses them.
- In Guided mode, hover over or tap any board space to see what it does.
- The ? button opens the rules; the robot button fast-forwards AI turns.

## How to play

1. Roll the dice and move around the Daily Grind. You collect your monthly cash flow every time you pass Payday.
2. Buy deals that add passive income, but keep enough cash for Splurges and layoffs.
3. When your passive income is greater than your total expenses, you move to the Freedom Lane.
4. Win by raising your Revenue Day income by the goal ($25,000 in Quick games, $50,000 in Standard) or by buying your dream.
